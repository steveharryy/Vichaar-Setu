# Vichaar Setu 💡

Vichaar Setu is a digital platform that connects students with investors to promote innovation and project growth. Students can submit their ideas and projects, while investors can explore and invest in promising innovations.

## 🚀 Features
- Idea and project submission by students
- Investor dashboard to explore innovations
- Project evaluation and funding opportunities
- Encourages startup culture and innovation

## 🎯 Objective
To bridge the gap between young innovators and potential investors, enabling ideas to grow into real-world solutions.

## 📌 Use Case
- Students with innovative ideas
- Investors looking for early-stage projects
- Startup incubation and collaboration

## 📌 Status
Concept / Development Phase
